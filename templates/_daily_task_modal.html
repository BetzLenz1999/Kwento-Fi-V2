Before you submit Each new post submission requires a 20 G$ on-chain contribution. It is non-refundable, including if your post is rejected, and it helps fund the shared reward pool. Your post link is not saved by KwentoFi until this payment is confirmed.

Rewards are set by the admin reward configuration, are paid only after review and approval, and depend on available pool funds. If your Celo wallet has at least 0.3 CELO, you pay your own estimated network fee. Below 0.3 CELO, the app may sponsor the estimated fee within its per-task, per-wallet, and daily limits; otherwise you will need to add CELO.
