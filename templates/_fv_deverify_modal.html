🪪 De-verify my account
This removes your face-verification status from this wallet on the GoodDollar Identity contract. It cannot be undone from here, but you can verify again later with this same wallet.
You will stop receiving daily G$
Daily G$, the daily voucher, and KwentoFi's earning features are for verified humans only. Once de-verified, this wallet cannot claim them until you verify again.
Your facial record is kept
This only removes the verification status on-chain. Your anonymized facial record stays with the GoodDollar identity service and is deleted automatically after the verification period (currently 180 days). This action does not delete it.
Useful before selling or giving away a wallet
De-verifying first stops an unused wallet from holding a verification slot that belongs to your face. Verifying again with your face is always possible on this wallet.
